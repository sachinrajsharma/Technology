QUESTIONS

Good questions.

What does Yugty Tech build?

Websites, web applications, Android and iOS apps, software, AI-powered solutions and digital transformation, with consulting to decide what to build first.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope, so no prices are published here. Tell us what you want to build and we will respond with a plan.

How long does a project take?

That depends on the size of the work, so we do not quote a standard timeline. After the Discover step you get a plan with milestones you can hold us to.

Will I see progress before launch?

Yes. We deliver in steps, and you can try working software at each one.

Do you offer AI tools for individuals?

Yes. The AI agents on this page, such as the Resume Expert and the Interviewer, are available now.

Who owns the code?

That is agreed in writing at the start of each project. Talk to us about your requirements.
