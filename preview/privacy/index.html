What we do with your details

This site has one form: the project brief on the Start a project page. This note says what happens to what you send.

What we collect

The name, email address, optional phone number and company, project type, budget range and message you type into the form. We do not use advertising trackers on this site.

How we use it

Only to read your brief and reply to you. We do not sell your details, and we do not use them to train AI models.

Who sees it

Your brief is emailed to our team through an email delivery service. People outside the team do not receive it unless you ask us to share it.

Your choices

Ask us to correct or delete your brief at any time by replying to our email.
